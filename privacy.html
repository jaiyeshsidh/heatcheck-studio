HeatCheck Studio – Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 24, 2026

HeatCheck Studio is a personal, single-user tool that its owner runs on his own computer to manage the YouTube channel "HeatCheck (Gameplay)". It is not offered to the public and has no other users.

What the app accesses

After the owner signs in with Google, the app uses the YouTube Data API and YouTube Analytics API to upload and schedule videos on the owner's own channel and to read that channel's performance data (views, watch time, comments).

How data is stored and shared

  • Sign-in tokens and channel data are stored only on the owner's own computer.
  • No data is sold, shared, or transferred to any third party.
  • No data is used for advertising.
  • The app does not collect data about anyone else.

Google API Services

The app's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Revoking access and deleting data

The owner can revoke access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions and delete the locally stored tokens by deleting the app's local data folder. Use of YouTube is also subject to the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy.

Contact

Questions: see the About tab of the channel at youtube.com/@HeatCheck-Gameplay.
